    Stop searching for leads. Let Loqi find them.

    Scroll to watch one search go from a whole market to a shortlist you can act on.

    1. Find

      Loqi searches the market for companies that fit the customer you described.

    2. Qualify

      Not every lead is worth your time. Loqi checks each company against your criteria and keeps the ones that fit.

    3. Research

      For each company that fits, Loqi gathers funding, hiring and market signals, and finds the right person to contact.

    4. Personalize

      Loqi writes a first message for each prospect from what it learned about the company and the person.

    5. Review

      You get a shortlist, the reasons behind it and a draft for every prospect. Nothing sends until you approve it.
